A simple way to bring store inventory online

Your shelves have
an online audience.

AnyThrift helps local secondhand stores make selected items discoverable online, without asking the store to digitize everything it owns.

📸

Selective cataloguing

Photograph chosen items and add structured details. Start with a manageable batch, not the entire store.

🏷️

Track every listed item

Each listing has a unique item ID. A staff scan records in-store sales and removes sold items from the online catalog.

🤝

Confirm before reserving

Customer requests enter a staff queue. The store verifies the item, then confirms a reservation.

🧾

Item-specific details

Capture clothing size, furniture dimensions, book details, electronics model and testing, and other category-specific attributes.

How the pricing concept works

One online price. Separate store pricing.

AnyThrift online price$32.00
Store's normal walk-in price$15.00

The website always displays the AnyThrift price. The store can still sell the item at its normal shelf price to a walk-in customer while it is available. When an AnyThrift request is confirmed, the store sets it aside; AnyThrift collects the online price and pays the store its separately agreed payout. The exact payout, returns, and operating terms must be agreed before launch.

Pilot proposal

Start small. Learn fast.

Proposed initial test: one or two partner stores, 10–20 selected listings, store approval for every item, a clear reservation process, and no requirement to digitize all inventory. Commercial terms, returns, payment, taxes, and store payouts are negotiated before launch.

Prototype boundaries: This is a local interactive demo. It generates printable Code 128 item labels, but has no built-in camera scanning, connected scanner hardware, user accounts, shared cloud database, live AI, POS connection, payment processing, live notifications, or production security. Changes stay in this browser. Sample listings and partner shops are fictional.
