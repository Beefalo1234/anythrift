For thrift and consignment shops

anythrift sells your best pieces online to buyers anywhere in the country. You get your full price. We do the photos, the shipping and the returns.

  1. We photograph a small batch of items you choose, about 10 to start, and list them online.
  2. A buyer orders one. We call or email to ask you to set it aside.
  3. We pick it up and pay your tag price on our weekly visit. We pack, ship and handle any returns.
  4. Local shoppers still come to you. Every listing names your shop, and locals buy at your price at your register.

What it costs you

Nothing to try. No contract for the pilot, no exclusivity, and you can take any item off the site at any time.
