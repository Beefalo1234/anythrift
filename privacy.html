What we collect

When you buy or place a hold, our payment processor, Stripe, collects your name, email, shipping address and card details. We see your name, email and shipping address so we can ship your order and contact you about it. We never see or store your full card number.

Our website host may count visits (pages viewed, rough location, device type). We don't use advertising trackers.

How we use it

Only to fill and ship your order, handle returns, and answer your messages. We don't sell or share your information, except with Stripe (payments) and USPS (shipping).
