A few of your items online. Shoppers come to you to try them.

We photograph and list a small, agreed number of items from your shop, and send interested shoppers to your counter to see them in person. They pay you, at your price, at your register. You keep selling everything else exactly as you do today.

Two ways it works

  • Shoppers visit you. They see your price online, we send them a pass, they try the item and pay you at your register. AnyThrift takes no payment in the store.
  • Shipped items are separate. If we sell one for shipping, we buy it from you at the agreed amount (your tag price plus $1) and handle delivery and returns.
  • Exposure to shoppers who never walk past your door.
  • No listing fee, no subscription, no exclusivity, no minimum.
  • Items that don't sell simply stay on your floor.

What we ask

  • Let us photograph selected items on your open days.
  • Scan the shopper's pass when they arrive, and scan our item label if they buy (one tap each).
  • Put an item aside only if you agree to a short try-on hold.
  • Tell us the same day if it sells in the shop.

How it works

  1. We agree a number of items per week (8–15 is typical).
  2. We photograph, measure and list them at one AnyThrift price.
  3. A shopper requests an item; we check it's still there with you.
  4. You hold it with our label; the shopper collects it and you're paid.

What we don't do

  • We don't touch your register or claim a POS integration.
  • We don't change your prices or your floor — in the store, your price is the only price.
  • We don't promise sales, traffic or a timeline.
  • We don't take returns back to you — we handle the customer.
  • We don't list anything you don't want listed.
  • We don't ask for exclusivity.

The trial

Two weeks, 8–15 items, no cost, no fee. Either side can stop with a week's notice. At the end we show you how many people we sent, how many arrived, what they bought and what you were paid. If it isn't worth your shelf, we stop.

Pilot terms are one page and marked for attorney review — we'll send it before anything starts.
