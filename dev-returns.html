Returns policy

Effective [DATE THE SITE GOES LIVE]. This policy applies to every item shipped by anythrift.

If the item isn't as described

Wrong size, a flaw we didn't show, or damaged in shipping: email us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. You get a full refund, including shipping, and we pay for the return label. No fees.

If you change your mind

Return it within 7 days of delivery, unused and in the condition it arrived. You pay the return shipping, and a $5 restocking fee is taken from your refund. Original shipping isn't refunded.

Lost packages

If tracking shows your package was lost, we refund you in full.

Holds for in-store pickup

Hold fees aren't refundable, except when the shop couldn't hold the item for you. Then you get the hold fee back in full.

How refunds work

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, within 5 business days after we receive the return (or right away for lost packages and missed holds). Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
