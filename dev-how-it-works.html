How it works

Every item is one of a kind and sits on a real shop's shelf in Westchester, NY, until you buy it.

  1. You buy. Your card is put on hold for the item and shipping. It isn't charged yet.
  2. We confirm it's still there. Within 1–2 days we check with the shop. If it's there, your card is charged. If someone bought it in person first, the hold is cancelled and you pay nothing.
  3. We pick it up and pack it. We collect items from the shops once a week and pack them ourselves.
  4. It ships within 7–9 days by USPS Ground Advantage, with tracking. Delivery usually takes 2–5 more days.

Why 7–9 days?

We don't keep a warehouse. Items stay at the shop, where local shoppers can still find them, until someone buys them. That's what keeps prices low and the pieces one of a kind.

Live nearby?

Every item page names the shop it's in. Go see it in person and pay the shop's own price at the register. If you want it set aside first, use Hold it for me: the shop keeps it for you for 48 hours.

Returns

Not as described? Full refund. Changed your mind? Return it within 7 days. Read the full returns policy.
