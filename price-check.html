Type in a store tag and what the same item usually sells for online. You get the price to list it at for shipping, or a "keep it in-store" verdict.

Example filled in: a $10 tag that usually sells for $30 + $8.50 shipping.

Settings (the decided rules)

How it decides The highest price you can list at is the market's delivered price × your %, minus your shipping charge. The lowest is the price that still leaves your minimum profit. If the highest is below the lowest, there's no price that works, so the item stays in-store only (walk-in or hold) or goes in someone else's box as an add-on.
